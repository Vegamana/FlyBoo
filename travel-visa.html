    Schengen tourist visa

    Spain visa checklist

    Document checklist and fee structure for a Spain (Schengen) tourist visa for Indian passport holders applying through BLS International.

    Official embassy fee
    €90 adult (~₹8,000) · €45 child 6–12 (~₹4,000) · free under 6
    Centre service charge
    BLS service charge ~₹1,800
    Standard processing
    15 to 21 working days after biometrics at BLS International

    Mandatory core documents

    • Schengen visa application form: printed, filled online (or in blue ink) and physically signed on pages 3 and 4.
    • Original passport: valid at least 3 months beyond your planned departure from the Schengen area, issued within the last 10 years, with at least two blank pages. Include clear photocopies of all pages, including blank pages and previous visa stamps.
    • Previous passports: original expired or old passports, with copies of all previous visas.
    • Photographs (3 copies): 35mm × 45mm, taken within the last 6 months, plain white background, neutral expression, no glasses.
    • Personal covering letter, signed: purpose and travel dates, day-by-day itinerary, details of anyone travelling with you, and who is covering the expenses.

    Travel and stay proofs

    • Confirmed round-trip flight reservations (to and from India, plus any internal Schengen travel).
    • Confirmed hotel vouchers covering every night of the stay across all Schengen countries visited.
    • Travel medical insurance: minimum cover of €30,000 (or $50,000) for emergency medical expenses, hospitalisation and repatriation. Valid for all Schengen states and for at least 15 days beyond the intended return date.

    Financial proofs

    • Personal bank statements: originals for the last 6 months, stamped and signed by the bank, showing a steady balance that covers daily expenses.
    • Income tax returns (ITR-V / acknowledgements) for the last 3 years.
    • Salary slips for the last 6 months, if employed.

    Occupational status

    • Salaried: employment contract or formal employment letter, plus NOC / leave sanction letter on company letterhead.
    • Self-employed or business owner: business registration certificate / GST / certificate of incorporation, company bank statements (last 6 months) and company ITRs (last 3 years).
    • Students: bonafide letter or NOC from the school or university, student ID card copy, and the parents’ financial documents, sponsorship letter and birth certificate.
    • Retirees: pension payment slips or proof of retirement income.

    If a minor is travelling

    • Birth certificate (original and copy) and passport copies of both parents.
    • Application signed by both parents.
    • Notarised affidavit / NOC: from the non-accompanying parent if travelling with one parent, or from both parents if travelling without them.

    Requirements, fees and timelines are set by the issuing authority and can change. Please confirm the current details with our team before you apply. FLYBOO.COM provides visa assistance and does not issue visas. The final decision rests with the relevant government or consular authority.

    Schengen (France) visa checklist

    Checklist for a Schengen tourist visa applied through the French mission and VFS.

    Visa fee
    INR 9,901
    VFS charges
    INR 3,500
    Processing time
    15 working days

    Passport and identity

    • Original passport valid at least 3 months beyond the date you leave the Schengen area, with at least two blank pages. Passports issued more than 10 years ago and handwritten passports are not accepted.
    • Passports with manual observations are accepted if you submit copies of the pages carrying the observation.
    • Attach all old passports, and copies of the first and last pages, visas, entry and exit stamps and any other inscriptions.
    • If you are not a national of your country of residence: proof that you are legally resident there (for example a residence permit).
    • If you hold an official travel document, a note verbale is required.

    Application form and photographs

    • One online visa application form (france-visas.gouv.fr), printed back to back and signed in columns 37 and 38, together with the registration receipt and checklist with barcode, for every individual application.
    • Complete the declaration with the application. Include your mobile number on the form, as the embassy or consulate can contact you at any time. The visa appointment is booked after the online form is filled.
    • Two recent colour photographs, 35mm × 45mm, semi-matt finish, 80% face coverage, white background, no border, not more than 3 months old, and not used in any previous visa.

    Covering letter

    • Covering letter with your name, designation, passport number, and the purpose and duration of your visit, signed by an authorised signatory with company stamp.
    • Address it to the office you apply through: The Consulate General of France, Mumbai; The Visa Officer, Embassy of France, New Delhi; The Consulate General of France, Kolkata, Puducherry or Chennai; or The Visa Officer, Consulate General of France, Bangalore.
    • Use company letterhead if self-employed, or plain paper if employed.

    Financials

    • Personal ITR for the last 3 years (last 2 years only if applying from Mumbai).
    • Personal bank statement for the last 3 months on A4 paper only (6 months if applying from Mumbai).
    • Salary slips for the last 3 months.

    Proof of occupation and status

    • Self-employed: company registration certificate or certificate of incorporation, and a brief company profile.
    • Employed: appointment letter and original leave letter on letterhead with the authorised signatory’s name, designation and company seal.
    • Freelancer: proof of occupation with financial documents.
    • Student: original NOC from the school or college with ID card copy.
    • Dependent: the main applicant’s documents apply.
    • Proof of socio-professional status (for example employment contract, certificate of employment, trade register extract, school attendance certificate or pension proof) and documents showing personal links to your country of residence (for example marriage certificate or family record book).

    Travel arrangements

    • Confirmed prepaid hotel reservation for the entire stay, matching the itinerary. The hotel should match the ticket dates, and the longest stay should be in France.
    • Confirmed air ticket (for a package tour, attach the detailed itinerary). Travel dates should be 7 to 10 days after you submit the application.
    • Original and copy of travel medical insurance for the stay, with cover of €30,000, taken from an authorised insurer and matching your travel dates.

    If a minor is travelling

    • If travelling with one parent or alone: an NOC from the other parent or both parents, as an affidavit on ₹100 stamp paper with a notary. Parents’ government ID copies are required.

    Notes

    • If you are travelling to France for the first time, biometrics are required. If biometrics were submitted within the last five years, the application can be submitted at France VFS.
    • If you booked a tour through a tour company, a tour certificate is required.

    Requirements, fees and timelines are set by the issuing authority and can change. Please confirm the current details with our team before you apply. FLYBOO.COM provides visa assistance and does not issue visas. The final decision rests with the relevant government or consular authority.

    Canada visa checklist

    Document checklist for a Canada visitor visa.

    FLYBOO service charge
    Professional support: INR 15,000 per applicant
    Includes
    Invitation letter and cover letter preparation, and round-trip flight bookings (for visa purposes only)

    Passport and identity documents

    • Current passport valid at least 6 months beyond the intended stay, and all old passports showing travel history.
    • Recent digital or passport-size photograph (35mm × 45mm, white background).
    • Aadhaar card copy, and copies of any previous visa refusal letters.

    Purpose of visit

    • Visiting family or friends: invitation letter from the inviter in Canada, a copy of their passport / PR card / visa, and proof of relationship.
    • Tourism: tentative travel itinerary, hotel booking details (if available) and an optional day-wise plan.
    • Cover letter: purpose of visit, duration of stay, who will fund the trip, and assurance of return to India after the visit.

    Financial documents

    • Self-funded: personal bank statements for the last 4–6 months, bank balance certificate, income tax returns (last 2 years), salary slips (last 3 months) or business income proof, and fixed deposits or investment proofs, if any.
    • Sponsored: sponsor’s bank statements, ID proof and status proof, sponsorship letter, and the sponsor’s financial documents.

    Employment or business documents

    • Employed: employment letter / NOC, leave approval letter, company ID card and salary slips for the last 3 months.
    • Self-employed: business registration certificate, GST certificate, company bank statements, and company ITR or audited financials.
    • Student: bonafide certificate or student ID, and leave approval from the institution (if applicable).

    Supporting documents

    • Marriage certificate (if applicable) and birth certificates (for minors).
    • Property or asset proofs (CA or CE report), LIC or investment proofs, and proof of family ties in India.
    • Previous visa copies and travel history.

    China Tourist Visa checklist

    Applications are submitted to the regional CVASC centre (New Delhi, Mumbai or Kolkata).

    Consular fee
    Single entry ₹2,400 · Double entry ₹3,600
    Centre service fee
    CVASC fee ~₹2,000–₹2,500 in addition
    Standard processing
    4 to 5 working days

    Mandatory core documents

    • Original passport valid at least 6 months from the date of entry, with at least 2 blank visa pages.
    • All previous passports (originals). If lost, an official police report / FIR with an explanation letter is required.
    • COVA application form and confirmation sheet: printed copy of the completed online application (visaforchina.cn), signed in ink on the confirmation page and the final declaration.
    • Passport photo (one physical copy), 33mm × 48mm: plain white background, taken within the last 6 months, ears exposed, neutral expression, no glasses, and dark clothing for contrast (avoid white or light tops).
    • Personal cover letter, signed: travel purpose, dates, day-by-day itinerary, and confirmation that the trip is self-funded.

    Travel and accommodation proofs

    • Confirmed round-trip or onward flight itinerary.
    • Confirmed hotel vouchers covering every night of the stay in China.
    • Day-wise itinerary showing the cities to be visited and planned activities.
    • If staying with a host: an invitation letter with the applicant’s details and stay details, signed and stamped by the host, with a copy of the host’s Chinese ID or residence permit.

    Financial proofs

    • Original personal bank statement for the last 6 months, stamped and signed by the bank.
    • A minimum balance of ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 per person, maintained consistently over the 6 months. Consulates strictly reject sudden last-minute lump-sum deposits.

    Occupational proofs

    • Salaried: employment verification / NOC on company letterhead, and salary slips for the last 3 months.
    • Self-employed or business owners: business registration (GST certificate, incorporation papers or Udyam) and company bank statements for the last 6 months.
    • Students: school or college ID card copy, bonafide / NOC letter, and the parents’ financial documents and sponsorship letter.
    • Retirees: pension payment slips or official retirement proof.

    Requirements, fees and timelines are set by the issuing authority and can change. Please confirm the current details with our team before you apply. FLYBOO.COM provides visa assistance and does not issue visas. The final decision rests with the relevant government or consular authority.

    Applications are submitted to the regional CVASC centre (New Delhi, Mumbai or Kolkata).

    Consular fee
    Single ₹2,400 · Double ₹3,600 · Multiple (6/12 months) ₹4,800 / ₹7,200
    Centre service fee
    CVASC fee ~₹2,000–₹2,500 in addition

    • Aadhaar card copy (front and back) as identity proof.

    Business and invitation documents

    • Official invitation letter from the Chinese host or company, issued by a trade partner, business entity or trade fair authority in China. It must include: the applicant’s full name, gender, date of birth and passport number; purpose of visit, arrival and departure dates, places to be visited and the relationship between host and applicant; and the inviting entity’s company name, official phone number, address, official red stamp or seal, and the signature of the authorised legal representative.

    Financial and travel proofs

    • Company or personal bank statements: originals for the last 6 months, stamped and signed by the bank.
    • A consistent minimum balance of ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 per person over the 6 months. Sudden lump-sum deposits before applying are flagged and rejected.

    United States visa checklist

    Document checklist for a U.S. visitor visa interview for applicants applying from India, including Hyderabad. Covers tourism, family visits and short business trips. Official requirements can vary by consulate, so follow the instructions on your appointment confirmation.

    Visa fee
    US$185 for the B1/B2 tourist visa

    Mandatory documents

    • Original valid passport.
    • DS-160 confirmation page with barcode.
    • Visa appointment confirmation letter.
    • Visa application fee payment receipt, if applicable.
    • One recent U.S. visa photograph (2 × 2 inches), if required or if the online photo upload fails.
    • Previous passports, if available.

    Supporting documents

    • Financial: last 6 months’ bank statements, latest income tax returns (if available), salary slips for the last 3–6 months if employed, and proof of savings, investments or other income.
    • Employment or business: employment verification letter, approved leave letter, employee ID card (if available); if self-employed, business registration, GST certificate and business bank statements, plus company financial statements or proof of business income where relevant.
    • Travel and family: tentative day-wise itinerary, proposed accommodation or U.S. address, invitation letter if visiting relatives or friends (optional), details of who will pay for the trip, marriage certificate and children’s birth certificates if relevant, and property or other evidence of ties to India if relevant.

    Additional documents by applicant type

    • Salaried employee: employment letter, salary slips, leave approval and company ID.
    • Business owner: business registration, GST certificate, business bank statements, ITRs and proof of ongoing business.
    • Sponsored trip: the sponsor’s financial documents and a clear explanation of who is paying.
    • Family application: individual passport and DS-160 confirmation for each applicant, including children.
    • Previous U.S. refusal: accurate details of previous refusals and the relevant application history.

    Requirements, fees and timelines are set by the issuing authority and can change. Please confirm the current details with our team before you apply. FLYBOO.COM provides visa assistance and does not issue visas. The final decision rests with the relevant government or consular authority.

    Turkey visa checklist

    Turkey tourist visa checklist for applications through Mumbai. The embassy may ask for additional documents, and all applicants need to book an appointment.

    Visa fees
    5,900/-
    VFS charges
    13,500/-

    Passport, form and photographs

    • Original passport valid at least 6 months upon arrival in Türkiye, with at least 2 full blank pages. If the passport was not issued in the jurisdiction of the Turkish mission you apply to, local address proof is required.
    • Visa application form filled by hand in blue or black ink in block letters, with 1 biometric photo affixed, signed by the applicant (or legal guardian for minors). Your signature should match on all documents.
    • Every applicant aged 18 or above must fill and sign the undertaking letter and bring the original with the application. Print this checklist and bring it to your submission.
    • Two biometric photos, white background, taken within the last 6 months, full face clearly visible without shadow, no glasses, 2.5 inch × 2.5 inch.
    • Copies of the first, last and visa pages of the passport; proof of civil status (Aadhaar card, PAN card, clearly visible); original previous passports (if lost, a police FIR document is required).

    Covering letters and invitation

    • Personal covering letter with the entire travel plan: purpose of stay in detail, accompanying persons, duration of stay, occupation and an expenses clause, signed with contact details and addressed to “Consulate General of Turkey, Mumbai”. A spouse and children travelling together must also submit personal covering letters. Travel dates on the letter must match all other documents.
    • Original leave letter on company letterhead stating the applicant’s designation, period of work, salary, purpose of visit, duration and travel dates, with signature, seal and stamp. Students: a no-objection letter from the school or official holiday scheme, with student ID card copy.
    • If visiting a Turkish citizen or a foreigner residing in Türkiye: an invitation letter with the applicant’s passport number and full name, a copy of the host’s Turkish identity card and passport or residence permit / visa copy, with address proof and contact number.
    • If an employer, proprietor, partner or director: company registration documents naming the applicant and company, and GST registration, both with original notary attestation. If you have no GST or Udyam certificate, an explanation is required.

    Monetary documents

    • Last 3 months’ salary slips on letterhead with original signature and seal / stamp.
    • Last 6 months’ salary bank statement and personal bank statement, with the bank’s stamp and signature on all pages. Bank balance must be a minimum of 2 lakh INR for one person. Statements should be updated to within at least the last 15 days, with the closing balance clearly shown (or a bank balance certificate).
    • ITR for the last 3 years (if you do not file ITR, give an explanation in the cover letter), and Form 16 (employers only).
    • Retired: retirement documents and income proof with the last 3 months’ personal bank statement. Freelancer: contract documents, leave / cover letter, proof of regular income and 6 months’ personal bank statement. Unemployed: an explanation of your source of income in the cover letter with proof of income.
    • Any educational, CA, bar council or doctor’s certificates must be submitted as notarised colour copies.

    Travel arrangements

    • Round-trip flight ticket. It is highly recommended not to buy a ticket before the visa is granted; a reservation is acceptable.
    • Hotel reservation. If booked through an agent or tour operator, include the applicant’s name and check-in and check-out dates on letterhead with seal, signature, name and designation.
    • Travel insurance covering the duration of stay in Türkiye (the policy can be provided at submission at the Türkiye Visa Application Centres).
    • Original authorisation letter from the applicant and travel agent if a representative or agent submits the application.
    • Tour itinerary (optional).

    Family and minors

    • Birth certificate copy with the original notarised document if a child under 18 is travelling, and an original school bonafide and NOC on school letterhead with signatory’s name, signature and school seal.
    • Minors need a notarised consent letter from both parents in all cases, with the parents’ original passports and photocopies attached. If a parent lives abroad, an NOC attested by the Indian embassy or consulate there, with a copy of that parent’s resident card or visa. The minor’s form is normally signed by both parents. If only one parent is the legal guardian (death or divorce), a notarised death certificate or court decision is required.
    • Marriage certificate with the original notarised document if a husband and wife travel together but are not named on the last page of their passports. A notarised affidavit with wedding card and photographs is accepted only for a marriage not older than 1 month.
    • If the husband sponsors the trip, a sponsor letter for the wife and children is required.

    Important notes

    • Address proof can be a registered or notarised rental agreement and a company letter. Voter ID, electricity or water bills and a police clearance certificate (PCC) of the current address may be requested.
    • All affidavits should be e-stamped and online verifiable. All letters must be typed (not handwritten), on letterhead, sealed and signed by an authorised person whose name, designation and contact details are clearly stated.
    • If a sponsor supplies the monetary documents, include an original sponsorship letter, the sponsor’s passport copy, and employment letter or notarised company registration, plus relationship proof if the sponsor is a relative.
    • You can apply for a Türkiye visa at most 60 days before your travel dates.
    • E-Visa eligibility: an applicant holding a UK, US or Schengen visa valid for the entire stay may be eligible for the e-Visa. The supporting visa must remain valid for at least 6 months from the date of the e-Visa application and also be valid during travel to Turkey.
    • If you travel with a family member or friend who holds a sticker visa or e-Visa for Türkiye, provide a copy of that visa with the co-traveller’s passport copies.
    • Rental income: notarised rental income proof and real estate registration documents are required if you submit rental income documents.
    • Damaged passports are accepted for submission only with a letter of acknowledgement from the applicant.
    • Group applications: notarised company registration documents should be submitted only by the main applicant. Other members of the group can submit a copy.
    • If a spouse is not shown in the passport and you travel together: a marriage certificate issued by the marriage registrar and attested by a notary public.
    • Passport holders of countries other than India, Nepal or Bhutan can apply at Turkish missions in India only with a valid Indian visa of at least 90 days’ stay or a resident / work permit in India, and processing can take longer.

    Requirements, fees and timelines are set by the issuing authority and can change. Please confirm the current details with our team before you apply. FLYBOO.COM provides visa assistance and does not issue visas. The final decision rests with the relevant government or consular authority.

    • Passport: current valid passport with at least six months’ validity (excluding blank pages), and all previous stamped and visa pages.
    • Australia visa application form, filled online. Also the Family Information Form, duly filled and signed, and the Authorization Form (Form 956A), duly filled and signed.
    • One recent passport-size photograph with matt or semi-matt finish, 60%–80% face coverage, white background and no border (35mm × 45mm).
    • Covering letter from the applicant with name, designation, passport number, and the purpose and duration of the visit, signed and addressed to The Visa Officer, Australian High Commission, New Delhi. On company letterhead with stamp and authorised signature if self-employed.

    Invitation and sponsorship

    • If visiting relatives or friends: an invitation letter from the relative or friend in Australia, with proof of their legal status (passport and visa copies), residence proof and employment proof. If they are paying for the visit, attach their financials (bank statement and salary slips for the last 6 months).
    • If sponsored: a sponsorship letter with the sponsor’s financial documents (personal ITR for the last 3 years, six months’ salary slips, and an updated personal bank statement for the last six months showing the bank’s name and telephone number clearly with sufficient balance).

    Travel arrangements (not mandatory)

    • Airline reservation: ticket itinerary, tour itinerary and tour confirmation.
    • Hotel confirmation.

    Financials

    • If employed: personal ITR for the last 3 years, six months’ salary slips, and an updated personal bank statement for the last six months with sufficient balance, showing the bank’s name and telephone number clearly.
    • If self-employed: company ITR for the last 3 years, company bank statement for the last six months showing the bank’s name and telephone number clearly, company registration certificate / certificate of incorporation, and optionally a credit card copy, foreign exchange endorsement or traveller’s cheque.
    • If a student: school or college ID card copy.

    Special cases

    • Children (under 18) travelling alone, or not with a parent or guardian: Form 1229, a consent / no objection letter signed by the parents, passport copies of both parents, the parents’ financial documents (personal ITR for the last 3 years, six months’ salary slips, personal bank statement for the last six months), and a birth certificate copy.
    • Applicants above 70 years: medical (travel) insurance of 12 months, and an “Aged Visitor Health Check” completed by a DIAC-appointed panel doctor.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do I need a visa for my destination?

    It depends on your nationality, where you’re going, why you’re travelling and how long you plan to stay. Tell us about your plans and we can help you work out what applies. It’s also wise to confirm with the official authority of the country you’re visiting.

    Which documents will I need?

    Required documents differ by country and visa category. A visa specialist can walk you through the checklist that applies to your trip.

    How long does a visa take?

    Processing times are set by the issuing authority and can change, so we don’t promise a fixed timeline. Apply well before you travel, and ask us about current estimates for your destination.

    What will it cost?

    A visa usually involves an official fee set by the government, and may also include service charges. We keep the two separate and clear so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

    Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

    No. Approval is decided solely by the government or consular authority of the country you are visiting. We can help you prepare your application, but we cannot guarantee the outcome.

    Is it safe to share my details in the enquiry form?

    The form only asks for the basics: where you’re going, your travel plans and how to reach you. Please don’t send passport numbers, passport scans or payment details through it.
